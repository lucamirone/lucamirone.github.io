Initial Advisory Assessment Share your objectives, timing, and the opportunity or challenge. Learn more →

Before an initial consultation, interested parties may complete this optional advisory assessment. The more information you provide, the better we can understand your objectives, prepare for the conversation, and focus the first consultation on the most relevant opportunities, challenges, and next steps.

This assessment is optional. If you prefer to make a general inquiry first, you may simply complete the contact form at the end of this page.

Consultation Discuss priorities, context, fit, and the most useful next step. Learn more →

The Consultation is the next step after the Initial Advisory Assessment. Once the assessment is reviewed, the consultation is used to better understand the client's priorities, current context, challenges, opportunities, and overall fit. The goal is not only to discuss what the client wants to accomplish, but also to identify what matters most, what may be urgent, and what practical direction can create the greatest value.

During the consultation, we discuss the information provided in the assessment, clarify the objectives, and evaluate the most useful next step toward the goals set. This may include defining immediate priorities, identifying potential obstacles, exploring strategic options, or outlining a practical path forward.

Advisory Brief Receive a focused scope with recommendations and engagement options. Learn more →

The Advisory Brief is prepared after the consultation and is designed to turn the initial discussion into a clear, focused direction. Based on the Initial Advisory Assessment and the priorities discussed during the consultation, the client receives a concise summary of the recommended scope, key considerations, and potential areas of focus.

The brief may include strategic recommendations, suggested next steps, and engagement options tailored to the client's objectives, timing, and level of support needed. It helps the client decide how to move forward with confidence through a targeted project, ongoing support, or a limited strategic review.
