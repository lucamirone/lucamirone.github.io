Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Luca Mirone Business Advisory respects visitor privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when visitors use www.lucamirone.com, submit a form, or provide information through the Initial Advisory Assessment.

2. Information Collected

Visitors may submit information directly through contact forms, advisory assessment forms, or messages. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or business name
  • Industry
  • Market interests
  • Business goals
  • Advisory assessment responses
  • Messages submitted through the contact form or Initial Advisory Assessment

If analytics tools are active, technical and usage data may also be collected, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the website
  • Referring website
  • General analytics and performance information

3. How Information Is Used

Submitted and usage information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Review advisory assessment submissions
  • Prepare for consultations
  • Communicate with potential clients
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Understand general website traffic
  • Maintain website security
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

We do not sell personal information.

4. Contact Forms and Advisory Assessment

When a visitor submits a contact form or advisory assessment, the information is used to understand the request and evaluate whether advisory services may be appropriate.

Submitting a form does not create a client relationship. A formal advisory relationship begins only after both parties agree to the scope, terms, and conditions of the engagement.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies, including Google Analytics or similar analytics tools when enabled, to understand website traffic, usage patterns, and website performance. Analytics tools may collect technical information such as device type, browser type, pages visited, referral sources, and approximate usage activity. Where consent controls are presented, analytics are intended to operate according to the visitor's selected preference.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use trusted third-party service providers, including website hosting providers, form submission services such as FormSubmit, email providers, analytics providers, and technical support providers. These providers may process limited information only to help operate the website, process communications, maintain technical functionality, or support related business operations.

7. Data Retention

Submitted information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential advisory engagements, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

8. User Privacy Choices

Visitors may contact Luca Mirone Business Advisory to request:

  • Confirmation of whether their information is held
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of submitted information, subject to reasonable business or legal requirements
  • No further contact

Requests may be sent to Luca@lucamirone.com.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect submitted information. However, no website, email system, or online transmission is completely secure, and no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

10. Third-Party Links

This website may link to third-party websites, including LinkedIn. Luca Mirone Business Advisory is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children under 13.

12. International Visitors

If visitors access the website from outside the United States, their information may be processed in the United States.

13. Accessibility

We aim to make this website accessible and usable for all visitors. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the website, please contact Luca@lucamirone.com, and we will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service in an accessible format.

14. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
